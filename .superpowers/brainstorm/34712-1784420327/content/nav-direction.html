🧭 Navigation Style

How should people explore the page?

A

Floating Pill

Glass-morphism pill that sits at the top, shrinks on scroll. Clean, unobtrusive. Current approach but elevated — glowing border, backdrop blur, subtle logo animation on scroll.

B

Sidebar + Dot Nav

Minimal fixed sidebar with a vertical dot indicator showing which section you're in. Ultra-minimal header, section progress tracked via dots. Feels editorial, artistic.

C

Hidden — Scroll-Reveal

No visible nav until you scroll up. The nav hides when scrolling down, reveals with a blur-in animation when you scroll up. Maximum immersion for the cinematic hero. Like how Apple's site works.
