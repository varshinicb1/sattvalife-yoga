📜 Scroll Narrative — How Sections Come Alive

Each section gets its own entrance personality

A

Cinematic Reveals

About: Image clips in from left, text slides from right with a 3D parallax tilt.
Courses: Cards fly in staggered like a dealing hand, each with a slight rotation settle.
Timetable: Map-style pins drop with a bounce.
Teachers: Circular portraits unfurl with a radial clip reveal.
Reviews: Cards slide in from alternating sides like a carousel.
⚡ Heavy on motion — best for desktop

B

Organic Zen

Every section fades in with a gentle upward drift — like leaves floating on water. No aggressive animations. The content itself (3D lotus, teacher portraits, course cards) does the visual work. Less is more.
⚡ Minimal, graceful, works on all devices

C

Morphing Layers

Each section subtly transforms the background — hero is dark & cinematic, About shifts to warm amber tones, Courses to deep green, Teachers to golden hour. Like walking through different rooms in a spa. Background gradients slowly shift as you scroll.
⚡ Rich atmosphere, heavier CSS paint cost
